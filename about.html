Our Story

A laboratory built on trust

Founded in 1973 and still delivering exceptional technical dental work over fifty years later.

Turner and Goldrich was founded by David Turner and Shraga Goldrich in 1973 and since that time has been specialising in crown and bridge work. We have over the years, become a highly respected and reputable laboratory and have maintained a long list of regular clients during those years for whom cosmetic work is our speciality.

After 12 years in Harley Street, the laboratory re-located to St. John's Wood, North West London. Preyesh Khatri has been with Turner and Goldrich since 2000 and has always maintained an important position at the lab and since October 2011 has become Managing Director, and recently relocated to Willesden Green.

The aim of the laboratory from the beginning was to produce high quality, custom-made dental appliances according to the required prescription, which will satisfy all technical, anatomical and aesthetic criteria.

We have a specialist team of dedicated Dental Technicians, all of whom are registered with the GDC and all of whom have over fifteen years of experience. Most of us have been working together for many years, and in combination with other supporting staff, we form a close-knit and extremely reliable work force that is Turner and Goldrich Limited.

The laboratory does work for dental surgeons throughout the London area, the British Isles, including the Channel Islands.

As we are a manufacturer of custom-made products, keeping up-to-date with the latest developments in Dentistry helps us maintain a high standard, especially as the dental field is constantly changing and evolving. It is our aim to grow and develop the laboratory by attending courses provided by top educators, introducing new products, materials and operating systems into the laboratory and thereby keeping ourselves on the cutting edge of high quality aesthetic dentistry.

About Our Clients

Partnerships that last decades

Our continued success is based on our provision of excellent service which is only possible through good communications.

We have a very loyal client base that has been established over the laboratory's 40 years of providing a consistent service. Our continued success is based on our provision of excellent service which is only possible through good communications between us and our dentists. The one's existence is dependent on the other and it is only together that we are capable of great things.

We welcome new clients to join us and our team in providing patients with restorations that they are happy with. Some cases require us working together from the very beginning, helping with treatment planning, materials to be used and ideas on the best type of restoration for each individual case.

We are also more than happy to come out to the surgery to see the patient in the chair to get a better idea of what the case entails. The mutual respect between ourselves and our clients with positive results achieves a mutual confidence and good foundation for a long term co-existence.

We pride ourselves in achieving long-lasting relationships built with our current clients, and endeavour to do so with all our new clients as well.
